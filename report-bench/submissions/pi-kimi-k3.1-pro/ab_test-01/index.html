Decision readout

Roll forward on speed. Gate the rollout on Dermatology.

The response-time improvement is consistent across the observed cuts. A single completion or retention story is not.

Speed ≠ completed care
01

Keep measuring the response-time win.

Treatment is faster on 39 of 42 days and in all five departments; the within-two-minute share rises +15.95 pp.

02

Do not call overall completion a win.

The observed difference is -0.08 pp, with a 95% CI from -1.03 to +0.87 pp. Investigate whether shorter consults affect any downstream quality measure.

03

Audit Dermatology routing and the pre-doctor queue.

Early loss rises +5.04 pp, and every Dermatology complaint group moves in the wrong direction. Review this segment before a broad rollout.

05 / METHOD

What was cleaned—and what was not assumed.

The page is a descriptive readout of the supplied user-level table. All metrics are reproducible with the expressions in facts.json.

01 · COHORT

One row per unique user

18,000 rows and user IDs. Group labels are trimmed before comparison, consolidating 19 labels with trailing whitespace. Mixed date formats are parsed into 42 daily periods.

02 · DENOMINATORS

Missing is not zero

Response and duration summaries skip missing values. All 903 missing responses align with pre-doctor loss; all 2,164 missing durations align with incomplete consultations. Follow-up excludes 186 missing values.

03 · ROBUSTNESS

Medians lead the time readout

Seven response times exceed one hour and seven consultation durations equal 640 minutes. Medians and p90s are emphasized so these extremes do not define the headline.

04 · UNCERTAINTY

Intervals, not causal proof

Rate intervals use a two-proportion normal approximation with a 1.96 critical value, unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Assignment and randomization details are not supplied.

Interpretation guardrail“Control” and “Treatment” are English labels for the two cleaned source groups. Complaint and department names are translated for readability. No missing outcome is imputed, and no outlier is deleted.
