How to read this report

A descriptive analysis of the supplied file data/measurements.csv: “Front and back pocket measurements of men’s and women’s jeans from popular brands.”

Sample

One row per jean: 40 men’s and 40 women’s pairs. After label cleaning, each of 20 brands contributes two styles per gender.

Measures

Pocket dimensions are shown in centimetres. Maximum and minimum values are reproduced as supplied; averages are arithmetic means.

Comparisons

Gaps are men minus women. Brand matching trims and lowercases labels. Shared-fit charts exclude men-only slim and regular fits.

Limits

This is a descriptive snapshot, not a weighted market estimate. Price correlations are not causal, and listed prices are not normalized.
