About this report

Small pockets.
Measurable difference.

A descriptive analysis of Front and back pocket measurements of men’s and women’s jeans from popular brands.

Definitions & calculations

front depth = (maxHeightFront + minHeightFront) / 2
front width = (maxWidthFront + minWidthFront) / 2

Back depth and width use the equivalent back fields. Percentage gaps use the women’s mean as the denominator. Brand means use two rows per gender.

The phone test compares 14.4 cm with the raw tallest and shortest front-height fields; it does not use averaged depth.

Sample & caveats

  • 80 rows: 40 men’s and 40 women’s jeans.
  • 20 brands after trimming whitespace and normalizing capitalization in brand labels.
  • Dimensions are presented in centimetres; prices reproduce the listed price values.
  • This is a small brand-and-style sample, not a representative survey of all jeans.
  • Group differences are descriptive; no causal claim is made.
