Interpretation

Response speed is only part of the journey.

Both the median and the upper-tail wait are lower in the test group. Yet the share leaving before a doctor is higher.

All 903 missing response times belong to users lost before a doctor. These timing statistics therefore describe users with a response, and cannot measure the waiting experience of those who left.

Dermatology loses more users before care.

The completion decline coincides with greater loss before a doctor. Review this department’s entry and handoff experience before interpreting faster responses as an overall service improvement.

Source & preparation

Source: data/data.csv. Group labels were trimmed, correcting 19 labels with surrounding spaces. Mixed date formats were parsed as dates. Department and complaint labels are translated into English.

Denominators & missing data

Completion and before-doctor loss use all users in each group. Timing and follow-up summaries exclude missing values. All 2,164 missing durations correspond to incomplete consultations. Follow-up is missing for 186 users; platform is missing for 6.

How to use these results

The file does not describe the intervention or assignment procedure. Treat group gaps as observed associations, not established causal effects. A follow-up flag does not establish clinical quality. Investigate Dermatology and validate the experiment design before a rollout decision.
