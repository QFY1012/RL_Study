READING THIS REPORT

Records, pairs & limits

Sources. Supplied tables: people.csv and convos.csv. All plotted measures are recomputed from people.csv. A row is a person in a conversation; repeated participant IDs mean these records are not independent people. The conversation table contains pair summaries, but the gaps here are calculated directly from individual ratings.

Definitions. “Affect” follows the source fields pre_affect, begin_affect, middle_affect and end_affect. The CSVs do not specify the question wording or when the stage ratings were collected. Stage spacing is schematic, not a timeline of measured minutes. Partner gaps are absolute score differences within a conversation. Perceived liking is you_like_me; actual liking is the partner’s i_like_you, matched by partner ID and conversation ID.

Missingness and interpretation. The trajectory excludes 55 incomplete records, leaving 3,257. Pair comparisons require both partners’ ratings, leaving 1,603 conversations. The liking comparison uses 3,206 matched records. Results describe this sample, with each eligible record or pair weighted equally. They do not establish that conversation caused the change, and no population uncertainty estimates are presented.
