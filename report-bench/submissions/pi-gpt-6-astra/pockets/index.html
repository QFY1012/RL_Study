01 / THE DIMENSIONS

A height gap, more than a width gap

Women’s mean maximum front height is 38.1% lower; front width is only 7.2% lower. The differences at the back are smaller still.

All bars share a zero baseline and a 30-unit scale. Values are group means of maximum measurements. Percentages show how far women’s means fall below men’s.

03 / HOW TO READ THIS

Measurements, not carrying capacity

This report uses the CSV’s maximum front and back height and width fields. Each jeans entry receives equal weight. “Height” refers to the recorded maximum height, not the opening or rivet height.

The CSV does not state measurement units, collection date, garment sizes or sampling method. Values are therefore labeled as recorded units. These dimensions do not establish usable area, volume or whether a particular phone will fit.

SOURCE & REPRODUCIBILITY

A balanced sample, a limited view

The supplied data/measurements.csv contains 80 entries. Brand names are trimmed and compared without capitalization, combining “7 for All Mankind” and “7 for all Mankind.” Style labels are trimmed for the skinny comparison.

Percent differences use men’s unrounded mean as the denominator. Displayed means are rounded to two decimals. The results describe this sample and should not be read as estimates for all jeans or a brand’s current range.
